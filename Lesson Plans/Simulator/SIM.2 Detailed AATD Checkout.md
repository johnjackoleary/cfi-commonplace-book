### Objective
Learn the advanced features of an [[AATD]] simulator. These features may be required for certain [[IFR]] training.

**Resources:** [[AC 61-136]]
**Builds On:** [[SIM.1 Basic AATD Checkout]]
**Schedule:** TBD

### Lesson Elements
- Instructor Operating System (iOS)

		> - Aircraft geographic location
		> - Aircraft heading
		> - Aircraft airspeed
		> - Aircraft altitude
		> - Wind direction and speed
		> - Turbulence
		> - Visibility
		> - Cloud cover 
		> - Dry, Wet, Icy Runway
		> - Seasonal Changes
		> - Wind/Rain/Snow
		>   
	> Failures included but not limited to:
		> - Engine
		> - Instruments
		> - Systems
		> - Avionics

	- Stratus seems to be the most consistent for cloud bases, if planning to break out on an approach
