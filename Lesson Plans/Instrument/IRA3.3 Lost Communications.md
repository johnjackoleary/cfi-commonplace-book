### Schedule
Ground 2 hours, Sim 1 hour

### Lesson Elements
- Many aspects of IFR clearances and procedures are in case of lost comms
	- "after... expect"
	- "5000' 5 min after departure"
- If we had perfect radio communication, these things would not be necessary
	- However, we need to plan for the situation of radios failing
- Concepts and examples in [[Lost Communications]]

![[IFR Radio Comm Part 1 General.jpeg]]
![[IFR Radio Comm Part 2 Two-Way Radio Failure.jpeg]]

#### Sim Activity
1. Flight plan from home airport to KPVF
2. Clearance including "climb maintain 3000', expect 4000' 5 minutes after departure" 
3. Simulated lost comms ~1 min after takeoff

### Completion Standards
Learner understands and can apply lost communications regulations.
