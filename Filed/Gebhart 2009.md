***Description***      :: Logging [[PIC]] flight time and cross-country time for [[IRA]].
***Author*** :: [[FAA Office of the Chief Counsel]]

#### Key Quotes
* In your example, Pilot A may log the entire flight (2.2 hours) of PIC flight time because that pilot was the sole manipulator of the controls for the entire flight. Pilot B may log the portion of the flight during which Pilot A operated in simulated instrument flight and Pilot B acted as the safety pilot (2.0 hours) because Pilot B was a required flight crewmember for that portion of the flight under 14 C.F.R § [[FAR 91.109 Flight Instruction, Sim Instrument, Tests|91.109]]{b).
* Because a [[safety pilot]] does not conduct the entire flight, a person acting as a safety pilot for a portion of the flight may not log any cross-country flight time for the flight.
